Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Get answers to common questions

Who can join the Engineering Physics Academy? +

The course is designed for first-year B.E./B.Tech engineering students from any branch who have Engineering Physics in their curriculum.

Which universities are supported? +

The demo syllabus selector lists SPPU, University of Mumbai, DBATU, RTMNU and Shivaji University. Course coverage has not been verified against the latest official syllabus for each university.

Are classes live or pre-recorded? +

Classes are conducted live every weekday from 8:00 PM to 9:00 PM IST. Recordings are available immediately after each session for review.

What happens if I miss a class? +

Recording availability depends on the academy publishing a recording. No video recordings have been connected to this demo yet.

How does the payment work? +

Yes. The payment page displays the supplied UPI ID and QR for a manual payment flow. After you share your transaction details with the academy on WhatsApp, the academy manually verifies and confirms the payment. This static website does not process or verify payments automatically.

Is there a certificate provided? +

A certificate preview is included, but issuance requirements and certificate availability have not been confirmed. The preview is not an issued credential.

Can I ask doubts during the course? +

You can try the local doubt form, which saves a note only in your browser. It does not send a question to faculty.

What devices are supported? +

Our platform works on all devices - smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktops. The 3D lab works best on devices with decent graphics capabilities.
